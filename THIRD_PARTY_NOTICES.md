## Reactive Resume

Resumetrics includes adaptations of the fifteen Reactive Resume templates
(Azurill, Bronzor, Chikorita, Ditgar, Ditto, Gengar, Glalie, Kakuna, Lapras,
Leafish, Meowth, Onyx, Pikachu, Rhyhorn and Scizor) from
https://github.com/AmruthPillai/Reactive-Resume (`packages/pdf/src/templates`).
Their layouts, names and descriptions are recreated for the Resumetrics editor in
`src/components/templates/ReactiveTemplate.jsx` and
`src/components/templates/reactive-templates.css`.
