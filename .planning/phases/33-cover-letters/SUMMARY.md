# PLAN-033 summary: cover letters

**Status:** Executed 2026-10-06. Automated checks and fixture checks pass; the signed-in walkthrough and the user-run AI evals are left for the user (same as earlier plans).

## What was built
- **Gate (GATE-01…05), revised 2026-10-06.** Job tailoring, Cover letters and Evidence check in the sidebar do not navigate: they are buttons that open a "Select a resume first" pop-up over the current page (dashboard included) with the page blurred (`ResumeGateDialog`, rendered by `Shell`; Esc, close button or a click outside closes it; focus is trapped and returned). It asks to start or import a resume and lists the resume in this session; `ResumePicker` takes an array, so saved resumes only change its source. A direct visit to `/workspace/tailor|letter|evidence` (or the old `/workspace/select/:tool`) without a resume goes to the start page with the pop-up open.
- **A letter on every template (CL-01…06).** `CoverLetterPage` renders the template's own markup and CSS (`ResumeHeader`, `.resume-columns-flow` with sidebar and main columns), so the masthead, photo, fonts, accent and column layout are the resume's. `letterDesigns.js` maps the 24 templates to the five families and `cover-letter.css` adds one device per family (rule, rail bar and hairline, offset hairlines, centred spacing). Contact-section templates (Minimal Academic, Simple Hipster) keep their contact block. Paper colour (Template / White / Warm / Stone), font, text size, accent are Format controls. One A4 sheet with a "Page 2" marker and a warning when it runs over; nothing is shrunk. Print rules in `print.css`.
- **Studio (CL-07…14).** `/workspace/letter`: `LetterStudio` in the resume editor's shell (resizable rails, undo/redo, zoom, export menu), NIMBUS and the Letter guide on the left, **Details | Format** on the right (Format switches in when text is selected). Inline editing of every part; name/contact edits write to the resume, the line under the name is the letter's own job title. `FormatPanel` was generalised (document selector, scope label, extra sections, element panels) instead of forked. Ghost hints from the ResumeWay guide for each empty paragraph (never printed). The **Letter guide** shows nine checks (role, company, addressed to a person, specific opening, measurable result, why this company, call to action, 150–450 words, adds to the resume) with an "Ask NIMBUS" fix button on each failure.
- **Signature (SIG-01…07).** Upload PNG/JPG/WebP; the paper colour is estimated from the border, paper becomes transparent, ink is recoloured, the result is trimmed (a shaded grey "photo" became a 9 KB PNG with alpha 0 corners). Clean-up, Size, Ink, Align, Remove; changing clean-up or ink re-renders from the stored downscaled original. Undo history keeps signature settings but not the picture.
- **NIMBUS on letters (LNM-01…08).** Same endpoint and turn runner, `document: 'letter'`. `shared/letterModel.js` (data, edits, operations), `shared/letterPlan.js` (validation: names, companies and addresses must come from the sources; fact guard with soft-skill and everyday-word allowances; generic openers, over-length and resume repeats are rejected so the model repairs once), `server/nimbus/letterEngine.js` (the ResumeWay rules as the prompt; asks why this company instead of inventing it). Own chat thread, starter chips, letter placeholders. A flat `{title, operations}` form is accepted for one-step edits because the 20B model often broke the nested shape. A live turn against Groq returned a valid four-paragraph letter after one repair.
- **Add to resume (ADD-01…07).** "Add to resume" returns to the editor with the letter as page 1 and a slim bar (Edit letter / Remove). The letter uses `letter-*` classes, so NIMBUS element lists, page counts and fit logic ignore it. PDF prints the letter first (page break), DOCX adds a page break, TXT prepends it; the studio also exports the letter alone. A **Cover letter** dock sits beside Tailor and Evidence in the editor rail; the tailoring bar has "Write a cover letter" once a job is analysed. Help has two new articles.

## Changes to the plan (recorded)
- The letter reuses each template's real markup rather than five separate grids, so the family mapping lives in `src/coverLetter/letterDesigns.js` (not the registry) and the families are small devices on top.
- The pure model is `shared/letterModel.js` (used by client and server) instead of `src/coverLetter/letterData.js`; `applyLetterOperations` lives there too. No `useCoverLetter.js`: state lives in `MainPage`, behaviour in `LetterStudio`.
- Paper "Template" means the template's own page colour; the Letter guide has nine checks (the plan's list plus the originality check).
